Birdverse

Build a star, grow planets, and help your flock farm, mine and build on them.

How to play
  1. Tap the Nebula, Asteroids or Comets to gather materials. Your birds gather too.
  2. Build a star, then a planet. Tap the planet and press Visit to land on it.
  3. Place farms, mines and houses on the planet. Move birds there and they get to work.
